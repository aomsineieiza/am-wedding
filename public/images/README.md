# Your Pre-Wedding Photos

You can drop your photos here in `/public/images/`:
- `hero.jpg` (Main photo under the clear umbrella)
- `envelope-couple.jpg` (Couple portrait on the envelope page)
- `gallery-1.jpg` to `gallery-6.jpg` (Gallery photos)
- `story-1.jpg` to `story-4.jpg` (Our Story photos)

Then in [`src/data/weddingData.js`](/src/data/weddingData.js), update the image URLs to:
```js
image: "/images/hero.jpg"
```
